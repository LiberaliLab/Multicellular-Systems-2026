# Course schedule

Each session is a hands-on block. Bring a laptop; Parts 1 and 3 run on Euler through
your browser, Part 2 runs locally.

| Session | Topic | Notebooks | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Setup** — Euler account, environment, data access | — | Euler + laptop |
| 1 | **What OME-Zarr is** and reading it with `ez-zarr` | Part 1 · 01–02 | Euler |
| 2 | **`ngio`**: containers, images, labels | Part 1 · 03 | Euler |
| 3 | **Plates and wells**: from 384 wells to one table | Part 1 · 04–05 | Euler |
| 4 | **Napari**: looking at the data | Part 2 · 01–02 | Laptop |
| 5 | **The experiment**: plate layout and the staining decoder | Part 3 · 00–01 | Euler |
| 6 | **Quality control and normalisation** | Part 3 · 02 | Euler |
| 7 | **Signaling** and **cell mechanics** | Part 3 · 03–04 | Euler |
| 8 | **Metabolism** and **organelles** | Part 3 · 05–06 | Euler |
| 9 | **Cell types and proportions** | Part 3 · 07 | Euler |
| 10 | **Integration** and group projects | Part 3 · 08, [Projects](projects.md) | Euler |

```{note}
Every chapter ends with exercises. The solutions are on the page, hidden behind a
dropdown — try the exercise before you open it.
```
