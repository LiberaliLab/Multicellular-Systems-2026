# Stage 3 — Going further

The four theme chapters each answered a question about *markers*. These two ask harder
questions, and they are where the course's methodological point lands.

**Chapter 07** stops asking how much of a marker a cell has and asks **what kind of cell it
is** — clustering on the lineage panel, naming the states, and watching one of them emerge
over 36 to 84 hours. It is also where the replicate unit stops being a warning and becomes
the method: the same comparison gives p = 10⁻¹⁸ counted over cells and p = 0.036 counted
over wells, and only one of those is about the treatment.

**Chapter 08** puts the four themes back together and tests the prediction the whole of
Part 3 has been building towards — does each perturbation move its own theme most? The
answer is partly no, and why it is no turns out to be more interesting than a clean yes
would have been.
